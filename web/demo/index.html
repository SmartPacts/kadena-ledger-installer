Install the Kadena app on your Ledger

Nano S Plus only · app version 1.3.3

This browser cannot talk to USB devices.

Use Chrome, Edge or Brave on a desktop computer. Firefox and Safari do not support the connection this needs.

Before you start:

  • Plug the Ledger in directly — not through a hub or monitor.
  • Unlock it and leave it on the home screen.
  • Close Ledger Live completely — it holds the connection.

You will never be asked for your recovery phrase. Anything that asks for your 24 words is a scam, including anything claiming to be us.
